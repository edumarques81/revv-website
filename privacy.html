Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 September 2026

Effective Date: 15 March 2026

1. Introduction

Revv ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Revv mobile application ("App") and related services ("Services").

By using Revv, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address — required for account creation and authentication
  • Name — optional, used for personalisation and shown to a coach you connect with

Revv does not ask for or store a profile photo. Where the App shows an avatar, it draws your initials.

2.2 Fitness and Health Data

  • Workout data — exercises performed, sets, reps, weight, duration, rest times
  • Body metrics — height, weight, body composition (if provided)
  • Activity history — completed workouts, personal records (PRs), progress over time
  • Location data — optional. Used only while you are looking at the "Nearby Gyms" screen, to find gyms near you. It is not tracked in the background and is not stored on our servers.
  • Apple Health data — optional. With your permission, Revv reads heart rate and workout data from HealthKit during a session, and writes completed workouts back to it. HealthKit data is used to run and record your workout; it is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with a third party for their own purposes.
  • Connected gym equipment — when you pair a machine over Bluetooth, the App receives live metrics from it (speed, cadence, power, resistance, incline).

2.3 Third-Party Integration Data

  • Strava data — if you connect your Strava account, we may import your activity history and sync future workouts
  • Google/Apple profile data — name and email when using social sign-in

2.4 Device and Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version — sent with each workout upload so we can diagnose problems
  • Crash and diagnostic reports, only if you have opted in to sharing them with developers in your device settings

Revv does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the App's privacy manifest declares no tracking domains.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Revv fitness tracking service
  • Personalise your workout experience and recommendations
  • Enable coach-athlete relationships (if applicable)
  • Sync data across your devices
  • Analyse app usage to improve our Services
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, and support
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

3.1 AI Coaching

Revv's AI coach generates its feedback by sending a summary of the workout you just completed — exercise names, sets, reps, weights, durations and the metrics recorded during the session — to a third-party large-language-model provider (currently Anthropic and OpenAI; see the table in section 4). That request is made by our servers, not from your device.

What we send is limited to the workout itself and the coaching context. We do not send your email address, your name, or any identifier that the provider could use to identify you. We do not permit these providers to train their models on the content we send them.

AI coaching is a feature of the App, not a medical service. See section 4 of the Terms of Service for the health and medical disclaimer.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored securely on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Sensitive data at rest is encrypted
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorised access

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Upon account deletion, your personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
  • Anonymised analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable usage data may be retained longer for product improvement

7. Your Rights (GDPR & Global Privacy Rights)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@revv.fit.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open the Revv App
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  3. Confirm deletion

Or email privacy@revv.fit with your deletion request. We will process your request within 30 days.

Note: Deletion is permanent. Workout data shared with coaches will be disassociated from your identity but may be retained in anonymised form for compliance purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Revv is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 16, and the App Store age rating reflects that. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending an email notification for material changes
